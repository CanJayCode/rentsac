# Rentsac Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** A phone-first PWA that replaces the father's Google Sheet: rent and light-bill tracking for 16 rooms, behind a shared PIN, English and Marathi.

**Architecture:** Next.js App Router with server actions. All money and billing rules live in small pure modules under `lib/` that are unit-tested. Data access sits behind a `Repo` interface with two implementations: in-memory (tests and local dev, needs no Supabase) and Supabase Postgres (production, server-side service key only). Balances are never stored: they are computed by replaying charges and payments through one `allocate` function.

**Tech Stack:** Next.js 15 (App Router, TypeScript), Tailwind CSS, Vitest, `@supabase/supabase-js`, Node `crypto` (scrypt, HMAC), `exceljs` (one-time import), Vercel.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-28-rent-app-design.md`

## Global Constraints

- Look is **Soft Light**: ivory background about `#f7f3ea`, deep green accent about `#1d6b3f`, amber for due, green for paid, white cards, soft shadow, rounded corners, large type, large tap targets. No spreadsheet look. Use the `design-taste-frontend` and `frontend-design` skills for every screen; no generic AI-template styling.
- Phone first, installable PWA. Works on a mid-range Android on mobile data.
- English and Marathi with a switch. Marathi terms: बाकी pending, जमा paid, रीडिंग reading, युनिट unit, भाडे rent, लाईट बिल light bill.
- Rupees are whole integers in code and DB. Light rate default 10 per unit, stored with each bill.
- One shared 6-digit PIN, no usernames. PIN stored only as a hash. Remembered 30 days via httpOnly cookie. Wrong guesses rate-limited.
- Supabase is never called from the browser. RLS on with no public policies. Service key only in server env.
- Free tiers only. No paid services.
- Dates are `YYYY-MM-DD` strings in the Asia/Kolkata calendar.
- Do not commit `.env*` files or the owner's Excel file.

## Review Focus

Inputs the spec implies that no feature task would otherwise test. Each has a test in the owning task.

1. A reading lower than the previous one (meter replaced or typo): warn, allow confirm, and restart from a new base so the bill is never negative. (Task 3, 11)
2. A reading equal to the previous one (zero use, as room 12 shows 944 three rounds running): saves as a ₹0 bill with no warning. (Task 3)
3. A payment larger than everything owed: the surplus is kept as advance credit and applied to the next charge, not lost. (Task 4)
4. A cycle start day of 29 to 31 in a short month, and a tenant who joins mid-period: periods clamp to the month's last day and never skip or duplicate. (Task 2)
5. Double submit of a payment or a round on slow mobile data: saves once. (Task 5, 10, 11)
